# Velaris

> Velaris is an AI-native Customer Success platform for mid-market and enterprise B2B SaaS teams. It centralizes customer data, turns interaction data into intelligence, predicts churn and expansion, and automates lifecycle execution so post-sales teams can operate at scale with AI agents and a Copilot workspace assistant.

## Product

- [Overview](https://www.velaris.io/): What Velaris is and how the platform works end to end.
- [AI Copilot](https://www.velaris.io/product/ai-copilot): Conversational AI assistant that answers account questions, drafts success plans, and analyzes portfolios in natural language.
- [AI Agents](https://www.velaris.io/product/ai-agents): Context-aware agents that monitor accounts for risk, handle meeting follow-ups, and manage sales-to-CS handovers autonomously.
- [View](https://www.velaris.io/product/view): Unified Customer 360 view, account hierarchies, and activity timelines that replace scattered notes and tools.
- [Analyze](https://www.velaris.io/product/analyze): AI-enriched health scoring, sentiment analysis, and churn/expansion signal detection.
- [Orchestrate](https://www.velaris.io/product/orchestrate): Automated, personalized customer journeys for scaling low-touch accounts without adding headcount.
- [Execute](https://www.velaris.io/product/execute): Success plans, playbooks, QBRs, and collaborative workspaces for standardizing CS execution.
- [Integrations](https://www.velaris.io/integrations): 70+ integrations across CRM, support, communication, product analytics, and financial systems; supports MCP for AI agent access to Velaris data.
- [Pricing](https://www.velaris.io/pricing): Plans and pricing information.
